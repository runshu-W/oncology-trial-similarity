\section{Configuration constants and reproducibility details}

\paragraph{Candidate features (9 dimensions).} As defined in the main text:
$s_i$ = Stage-2 overall similarity $/100$; disease, regimen, endpoint,
follow-up, and eligibility match scores = the corresponding Stage-2 $0$--$5$
dimension scores $/5$; result-quality score $/5$; negative red-flag severity
$-\mathrm{rf}_i \in [-1,0]$; and $\log(1+n_i)$. Stage-2 dimension scores are
produced by the deterministic reranker released with the code
(\texttt{pipeline/oncology\_trial\_similarity\_pipeline.py}); the scoring
rules, keyword tables, and gate conditions are implemented there verbatim and
exercised by the released unit tests.

\paragraph{Gate and rule prior.} The conservative gate multiplies factors for
endpoint compatibility ($<1.5/5$ blocks), result usability ($0$ blocks),
disease match ($<1.5/5$ scales by $0.2$; $<2.5/5$ by $0.6$), and low-severity
red flags (scale $0.5$). Rule discounts $a^{\mathrm{rule}}_i$ follow the
suitability ladder $0.75/0.40/0.15/0$; the rule prior fixes $\lambda_0=0.2$
with a flat $\mathrm{Beta}(1,1)$ anchor.

\paragraph{Selective model.} DeepSets encoder $\phi$: $9 \to 16 \to 16$
(ReLU); score head $\rho$ and discount head: $32 \to 16 \to 1$; weak-component
head $\psi$: $16 \to 16 \to 1$ on the pooled context. Training: Adam, learning
rate $0.01$, $100$ epochs, full batch, seed $20260603$ (primary and every
window refit); KL-to-rule weight $\rho = 0.1$ on normalised candidate
allocation; listwise term disabled ($\eta = 0$); borrowed-information penalty
$10^{-4}\max(\sum_i \lambda_i a_i n_i - 100, 0)^2$. EB anchors by
beta-binomial MLE (grid plus coordinate refinement): training split
$\mathrm{Beta}(0.68, 1.70)$; past-only fits $\mathrm{Beta}(0.65,1.61)$,
$(0.68,1.70)$, $(0.69,1.72)$ at the 2020/2021/2022-12-31 cutoffs.
Disease-stratified EB: per-primary-site MLE for strata with $\ge 40$ training
queries, global fallback otherwise.

\paragraph{Evaluation constants.} Clean test split: deterministic $80/20$
(seed $20260603$), $n = 281$. Disjoint forward windows by primary completion
date: $(2020\text{-}12\text{-}31,2021\text{-}12\text{-}31]$ $n=181$,
$(2021\text{-}12\text{-}31,2022\text{-}12\text{-}31]$ $n=204$,
$>2022\text{-}12\text{-}31$ $n=390$; past-only training pools $632/813/1{,}017$.
Paired bootstrap: $4{,}000$ resamples, percentile intervals. PIT: randomized
form, RNG seed $20260707$; prediction intervals: equal-tailed on the exact
beta-binomial mixture pmf. Pre-specified information subgroup: $I = \sum_i
\mathrm{gate}_i a^{\mathrm{rule}}_i n_i \ge 8$, fixed from exploratory
analysis of the two earlier windows before the final window was examined.
Robust-MAP baseline: DerSimonian--Laird heterogeneity on the rate scale,
Haldane-smoothed rates, MAP ESS cap $200$, $\epsilon = 10^{-4}$,
robustification weights $w \in \{0.5, 0.9\}$. SAM adapter: temperature
$\tau = 1$; multiplier $\min(1, R^{\tau})$ with $R$ the
historical-to-weak predictive ratio at the observed outcome.

\paragraph{Gold-standard simulation.} As released in
\texttt{simulation/gold\_standard/}: $2{,}000$ replicates per cell (estimation
and design worlds), simulation training set $300$ queries, $60$ epochs, seed
$20260720$; design test $H_0: \theta \le 0.20$ at one-sided $0.025$,
alternative $\theta_0 = 0.35$; conflict grid $0$--$1.5$ logit shift; success
threshold $0.975$; fixed-discount comparator $0.35$. Data-generating
constants, trap/hidden-gem composition, and per-cell outputs are archived in
\texttt{results/tables/gold\_standard\_*}.

\paragraph{Sensitivity artifacts.} Repository artifacts include section-weight
sensitivity (\texttt{results/tables/section\_weight\_sensitivity.csv}),
feature ablation, $\lambda_0$-frontier and conflict sweeps from the
operating-characteristics engine, and the per-example evaluation rows behind
every table in this revision
(\texttt{artifacts/rerun\_unitfix\_2026-08-14/} and the selective-revision
artifact set); regeneration commands are given in
\texttt{docs/reproducibility.md} and the figure scripts assert every plotted
headline value against the manuscript numbers before writing output.
