\section{Claim--Evidence Map}

This table maps each principal claim in the main text to its supporting
evidence and the boundary of that evidence. It is a cross-check aid: every
``Status'' entry states the setting in which the claim is supported (for
example, retrospective ORR, leakage-free, simulation, or borrowing-layer only)
and does not assert clinical or regulatory validity. All real-data values
correspond to the corrected-units re-run on $N=1{,}407$ leakage-controlled ORR
pseudo-queries; simulation values are unaffected by the endpoint-unit
correction.

\begin{longtable}{p{0.32\textwidth} p{0.39\textwidth} p{0.19\textwidth}}
\toprule
\textbf{Claim} & \textbf{Evidence} & \textbf{Status} \\
\midrule
\endhead
Trained two-head calibration improves held-out NLL over rule. & $2.8957$ vs
$3.0100$ mean NLL. & Supported (retrospective ORR). \\
SAM conflict adaptation improves NLL and responds to conflict. & Two-head-SAM
$2.7938$, rule-SAM $2.9006$; simulation conflict behaviour. & Supported as
sensitivity evidence. \\
Conflict-adapted borrowing shows lower future-fold NLL in three rolling-origin
splits. & Leakage-free rolling-origin: rule-SAM $-0.100$ to $-0.109$;
per-fold-retrained two-head-SAM $-0.198$ to $-0.203$ nats (bootstrap 95\% CIs
exclude 0). & Supported (leakage-free) for closed-form and learned models. \\
Priors show improved (not perfect) calibration, not merely lower loss. & PIT KS
$0.177$, 95\% coverage $0.996$, reliability slope $1.36$ for two-head-SAM. &
Supported (held-out ORR). \\
Learned prior outperforms a standard robust prior. & Two-head-SAM $2.794$ vs
best robust-MAP $3.002$; robust-MAP degrades to $3.100$ at higher
robustification weights. & Supported (retrospective ORR). \\
Borrowing improves design-based operating characteristics without conflict. &
Gold-standard simulation: type I error $0.025$ vs $0.037$ no-borrowing; power
$0.587$ vs $0.536$; full pooling $0.584$. & Supported (simulation). \\
Under strong conflict the learned prior degrades and is not the safest. & Type
I error $0.070$ vs rule $0.050$ and robust-MAP $0.047$, without compensating
power gain. & Supported (simulation; reported as a limitation). \\
Design-time signals cannot detect uniform whole-set conflict. & Scenario S4:
every design-time method below chance (ROC-AUC $0.445$); outcome-dependent
UIP-JS $0.839$. & Supported (simulation; structural limitation). \\
The prospective signal does not improve discrimination but is necessary for
the discount head to respond. & Ablation: ROC-AUC change $-0.000$ to $+0.016$;
without $g_i$ the fitted discount is constant (SD $0.002$) and borrowed
information is flat across donors. & Supported (simulation ablation). \\
Adaptive borrowing protects type I error with drifted external controls. &
External-control scenario: pooling $0.211$, internal-only $0.014$, learned
prior $0.032$. & Supported (simulation). \\
Extracted observations are of measured quality. & About 44\% of raw arm rows
are not valid binary observations; the admission rule (synthetic round-trip
8/8) removes them before prior construction. & Supported (July audit;
pre-correction corpus). \\
The endpoint-unit defect is quantified and corrected, and results were
regenerated. & $804/3{,}319$ arm-level ORR rows provably wrong pre-correction;
all four call sites fixed; $1{,}470 \to 1{,}407$ examples; full re-run with
fixed seeds and pinned environment. & Supported (correction documented in
main text). \\
Reported two-head results use genuine, freshly trained checkpoints. & Corrected
re-run trained the primary and per-fold models from scratch (seed $20260603$);
attached predictions match training-run NLL rows to within $10^{-4}$. &
Supported. \\
